Locking the screen starts the time away at once, and alerts stay quiet while it's locked; 3 minutes locked is a break, a shorter lock is nothing. Typing the password, or a jiggler, under the lock screen doesn't end a break. The main agent's own activity (the prompt, its replies, its tool calls as each starts and ends) counts as you being there, so watching it work isn't a break, but 3 minutes with nothing from you or the main agent is, mid-turn too. A subagent's work doesn't count. A break's length counts from your own last input.
