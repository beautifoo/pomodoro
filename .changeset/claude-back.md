When you come back from a break, a toast says how long you were away and which pomodoro of the set you're taking up, as `🍅 Back after 4m · pomodoro 2 of 4`, or after a long break `🍅 Back after 22m · new set, pomodoro 1 of 4`, and from 2 hours away in hours, as `🍅 Back after 14h`. It comes once, with no banner and no chime, even with several sessions open.
