When you come back from a break, an info line says how long you were away and which pomodoro of the set you're taking up, as `🍅 Back after 4m · pomodoro 2 of 4`, or after a long break `🍅 Back after 22m · new set, pomodoro 1 of 4`. It comes once, with no chime, even with several sessions open.
