`/pomodoro quiet` is for a meeting, a demo or pairing: the count goes on, shown as `🍅 quiet · 2 of 4 · 7/25m`, but there's no alert, no chime, no status-line warning and no check-in, and sitting still is never a break. It ends when you run `/pomodoro quiet` again, or lock the screen or close the lid for 3 minutes; if a break is due then, the alert comes at once. It's shared by your Claude Code sessions, and runs at once even while Claude is working.
