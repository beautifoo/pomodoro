A break is now 3 minutes or more away. A stray key, a click or a keep-awake app's mouse jiggler neither holds a break off nor ends one: input counts once macOS's idle time, looked at every 10 seconds, shows it in three looks within a minute. Off macOS and over SSH, 3 minutes without a prompt, a reply or a tool call of the main agent is a break. The osascript that watched the input all along is gone.
