Pomodoros come in sets of four. At the end of the fourth, the alert, the warning and the note to the agent ask for a 15 to 30 minute break instead of a short one, and keep asking until you take it: a short break then doesn't start a new set. 15 minutes or more away, a closed lid or no session open included, starts a new set. The count shows the pomodoro under way in the set and the minutes into it, as `🍅 3 of 4 · 12/25m`, and goes on as `5 of 4` after the fourth; a pomodoro cut short by a break doesn't count. The alert asks for 3 to 5 minutes away.
