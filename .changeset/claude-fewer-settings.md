The pomodoro and break lengths, the mouse-movement setting and the check-in switch are gone: a pomodoro is 25 minutes, a break 3 minutes or more, and the check-in is always on. Values left in settings.json are ignored. There is no osascript banner any more, so where the terminal can't raise a notification (Apple Terminal) the alert is the one in the app, with the chime.
