# The paper's rhythm, without timing settings

The plugin had a rhythm of its own: a pomodoro and a break of the lengths the person set (`workMinutes`, `breakMinutes`), with no sets and no long break, and it got in the way a few times a day, with a chime in a meeting or a break cancelled by a key. It now follows Cirillo's paper (_The Pomodoro Technique_, v1.3, 2007) and accepts the ranges the paper allows instead of asking for numbers: a pomodoro is 25 minutes, 3 minutes or more away is a short break, 15 or more a long one, and after a set of four the alert asks for a 15 to 30 minute break. `workMinutes`, `breakMinutes`, `moveSeconds` and `checkIn` are gone, and their values are ignored; `sound` and `emoji` stay. The check-in is always on: without it the plugin has no reason to exist. A meeting is something the person tells it, with `/pomodoro quiet`; it never detects one through the microphone, the camera or the calendar. The main agent's own activity (a prompt, its replies, its tool calls) counts as the person being there, so watching it work isn't a break; it holds a break off but doesn't end one, though a prompt the person types is their own input, and does. A subagent's work doesn't count, and a break's length runs from the person's own last input, not the agent's. Where there's no system idle time to read (Linux, Windows, SSH), the agent's activity stands in for input: it ends a break too, and the break runs from it. There's no `osascript` any more: the input idle time and the screen lock come from `ioreg`, every 10 seconds and every minute, and there's no fallback banner, so where the agent can't raise one (Pi, Apple Terminal) the alert is the agent's own and the chime.

## Considered Options

- **Keep the timing settings**, with sets and a long break on top: more to set, and a length the person picks once doesn't fit the breaks they actually take; the paper's ranges take any of them.
- **Learn the lengths from use**: the plugin would drift toward the 90-minute stretches it's there to break up.
- **Detect meetings** by the microphone or camera in use, or the calendar: that's listening, which Lorenzo rejected. Quiet is told, and ends by itself with a lock or a closed lid.
- **The person's input alone as presence**: watching a long turn would end the pomodoro. **A subagent's work too**: its long runs would hold off every break. **A break from the agent's last activity**: someone who left before a long turn ended would be credited with a shorter break than they took, perhaps a short one for a long one.
- **Keep osascript**, a watcher that read the mouse four times a second and a banner macOS credited to Script Editor: a long-running process per session for what a look at `ioreg` every 10 seconds tells, now that a stray nudge never counts anyway.
